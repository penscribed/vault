Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Nnabuike Atuogba · nnabuikejoe@gmail.com

Who we are

Vault is a personal and business money-tracking app published by Nnabuike Atuogba ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Vault handles and how. Effective date: 29 October 2026. Contact: nnabuikejoe@gmail.com.

The short version

Vault has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We cannot see, collect, sell or share your financial records, because they never reach us.

Information stored on your phone

• Income, expenses, categories, notes and tags you enter • Savings goals, investments, debts, budgets, recurring entries and subscriptions • Names of businesses and people you record (for example, who owes you money) • Receipt photos you choose to attach • Bank alert messages, only if you turn on bank-alert reading (see below) • Your settings and a scrambled (hashed) form of your PIN — never the PIN itself This information is stored in an encrypted database and encrypted files inside Vault's private storage. Other apps cannot read it. Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for Vault.

Bank SMS alerts (optional)

Bank-alert reading is OFF until you switch it on in More › Bank alerts and grant SMS permission. When it is on, Vault reads incoming and recent text messages on your phone to find bank debit/credit alerts and turn them into suggested entries. Messages that do not look like bank alerts are ignored and never stored. Bank alerts that are kept are stored encrypted on your phone only. Vault never sends SMS, never uploads messages and never shares them. You can switch this off at any time; you can also remove the permission in Android Settings.

Camera and photos (optional)

Used only when you choose to attach a receipt photo. Photos are shrunk, their location and camera details are removed, and they are encrypted inside Vault. Vault does not access your gallery except for the photo you pick.

Fingerprint unlock (optional)

Fingerprint checks are performed by Android. Vault only receives a yes/no answer and never receives or stores fingerprint data.

Notifications

Used for budget alerts, new bank alerts to review and app updates. Details are hidden on the lock screen. You can turn notifications off in Android Settings.

Internet use

Vault connects to the internet only to check GitHub (github.com) for new versions of the app and to download them. No personal or financial information is sent. As with any website, GitHub can see your device's IP address and that a request was made; GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that. You can turn automatic update checks off in More › App updates.

Backups, reports and sharing

Backups and PDF reports are created only when you ask. Backups are encrypted with a password you choose. When you send a backup or report by email or another app, that app or provider handles it under its own privacy policy. Keep your backup password safe — we cannot recover it.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data, track you, show ads, sell or share data with third parties, or use your data to train any system. There are no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools in Vault.

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete everything at any time in More › Privacy & legal › Delete all my data, or by uninstalling Vault. Deletion cannot be undone unless you have a backup.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, the UK GDPR or the EU GDPR), you have rights to access, correct, export and delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Because your data is held only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app: view and edit any record, export with Backup or PDF reports, withdraw SMS/camera/notification permissions, and delete all data. For any question or complaint, contact nnabuikejoe@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data-protection authority (in Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission).

Children

Vault is intended for adults managing their own or their business's money. It is not directed at children.

Security

Vault uses AES-256 encryption, keys protected by the Android Keystore, PIN and fingerprint lock, automatic lock, screenshot blocking and optional erase after repeated wrong PINs. No method is perfect; keep your phone, PIN and backup password secure.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and at https://penscribed.github.io/vault/privacy.html. Significant changes will be shown to you in the app before you continue using it.
